      Missable trophies — do these on your first pass

      “Missable” here means tied to one moment or one attempt: a single scene, a choice, or a chain you must complete without breaking it. Story chapter and mission trophies are not flagged missable — finishing the chapter or mission earns them, and a missed mission moment can be replayed. Nothing here is claimed to be permanently missable; the flag tells you when to pay attention.
