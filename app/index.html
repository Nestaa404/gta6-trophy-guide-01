    Roadmap to the platinum

    Six stages, in the order to play them. Story first — the missable trophies all live there — then side strands, collectibles and activities, then the combat, chaos and money trophies, and finally 100% completion, which pops the platinum.

    Missable trophies — read this before you play

    16 of the 55 trophies are tied to a single story moment, a partner moment, or a choice that only comes up once. None of them needs a special build — they need timing. The story ones are safe as long as you play the missions in order and use mission replay if you fail one; the partner and choice ones need you to act while the moment is live.

    About this guide

    Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation trophy list, as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles. Every guide, step and roadmap stage on this page was written for this entry, in its own words, from those official descriptions — and each guide names its sources beside it, so you can see where every fact comes from. No Rockstar Games logo, artwork or game screenshot is used anywhere in this guide.

    Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and never leaves your device unless you export it. The guide is a set of static files: it works with the network off.
